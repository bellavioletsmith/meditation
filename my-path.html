Hey, I’m Bella Smith*

My formal meditation practice began 4 years ago when I found myself in immense and unrelenting grief. 

As my practice deepened, I sought out longer-form practice and spiritual teachers. This led me to Thailand, where I studied alongside monks at Wat Sopharam under Ajahn Puttar, a venerable Vipassana teacher in the Mahasi tradition. 

From there, I returned to NYC, where I busied myself with pursuing a childhood dream. Though, it wasn’t too long until my meditation journey started to eclipse my regular life. 

At the end of 2024, I pulled out of life in NYC to answer the call of my practice.

What ensued was a year in meditation — where I was supported by a contemplative residency @ Huber Sanctuary, the guest student program @ Insight Meditation Society, and a month-long silent retreat @ Spirit Rock.


In tandem, I speed-ran an education in complex trauma (C-PTSD): its developmental origins and modalities of healing. This included multi-month studies on dysfunctional family systems, narcissism (esp. covert narcissism, co-narcissism + narcissism dynamics, & abuse), inner child and parts work, MDMA & plant medicine therapy, and somatic (nervous-system) models of trauma and healing.  

I am especially keen on understanding how contemporary frames of complex-trauma recovery map onto meditation map theory — specifically the dukkha nanas & traditional Buddhist conceptions of the path.
